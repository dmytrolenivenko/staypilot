Each rate is a small 2021 anchor carried forward by INE's Índice de Custos de Construção de Habitação Nova, which INE publishes monthly and splits into labour and materials — so a concrete pool and an imported lift escalate at their own rates rather than at one blended number.

Everything else is a ratio of one anchor: a standard build was about €1,000/m² in 2021, a concrete pool is 0.82 of the house rate per m² of water, a garage bay 0.55, a finished garden 0.041. Ratios do not go stale — what a pool costs relative to a house does not move with inflation, only the price of both does. Solar is the exception and is quoted flat: panels got cheaper since 2021 while building got dearer.

The headline carries a −15% / +25% band, deliberately lopsided: estimates of this kind miss low far more often than high, because scope grows, ground surprises you, and finishes get upgraded halfway through. It is a projection, not a quote.
