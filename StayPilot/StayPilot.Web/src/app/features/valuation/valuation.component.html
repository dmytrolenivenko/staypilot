Every number here comes from asking prices, not sale prices — what a property would be advertised at today. What it sells for is lower, and nothing in the database says by how much.

Click any row to open it. Inside are the adverts the estimate was checked against, what each feature contributes, how long homes sit in that place, and three paths for the years ahead.

The projections blend two rates that are kept apart on purpose: a seeded long run assumption for the district, and the trend measured from the adverts nearby. The seeded one is an assumption, not a measurement, and the panel says so in the words it was written with.
