Median and average are both shown on purpose: the gap between them is the reading. They agree on an even market, and the average runs away from the median exactly where a few expensive listings are pulling it.

The distribution bars are as wide as the price range they cover, drawn between the 5th and the 95th percentile so one very expensive listing cannot stretch them until every other bar is empty — that listing is still counted, in the last bar.

Inside … is the section to read on a broad slice. A single median taken across every distrito describes no market you can buy in; the same listings split by place do. The split is always one grain finer than what you picked — distritos when nothing is narrowed, municípios inside a distrito, freguesias inside a município — and vs slice says how far each place sits from the median printed above it. Click any row to narrow the whole screen to that place.
