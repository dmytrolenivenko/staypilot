Every place row names itself and then, underneath, the places it sits inside — Guia with Albufeira · Faro beneath it. The chip says which grain the row measures: a distrito is the broadest, a município is a council inside one, a freguesia is a parish inside that.

The middle value rather than the average, so one very expensive villa cannot carry a whole town. Click any column to sort; click it again to flip. €/m² switches between the most expensive and the best value places.

Deals is how many listings are asking at least 10% below what the valuation model thinks they are worth — under-priced, not cheap. A whole town being inexpensive is not a bargain; one flat below its own worth is. It sorts on the share, not the count, because 40 out of 2,000 is worse hunting than 8 out of 40.

Listings is the evidence behind the price. Rows marked thin rest on fewer than 15 adverts — still true, but a lead rather than a finding, so read them with suspicion before acting. Raise Min listings to drop them entirely. The numbers are worked out on the server, so they are only as fresh as the last recalculation.
