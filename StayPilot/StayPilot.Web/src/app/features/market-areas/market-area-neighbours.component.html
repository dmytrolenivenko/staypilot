Pairs of places close enough to be a real choice, priced far enough apart to be worth the move. Biggest gap first. This is where the money actually is: a border you cannot see on a price list.

Distances are measured between the middle point of each place's listings, not between real borders — we hold no boundaries. So read 3 km as roughly how far you would move, not as an exact figure. Two places can also sit close on the map and still be separated by water or a motorway, which no distance here knows about.
