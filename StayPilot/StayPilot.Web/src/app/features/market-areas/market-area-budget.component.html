The other way round from every property portal: instead of filtering places by price, put in what you have and see what it actually gets you in each one. Same money, very different homes.

“Best fit” is the most rooms a place usually sells for your budget, judged on what that typology typically costs there — not on the cheapest advert in it. One bargain T3 in Cascais does not make Cascais a place where €300,000 buys a T3. Places where the budget reaches nothing are left out rather than shown empty.

The three number columns will not multiply out, and that is correct: each is its own middle value taken separately, and the middle price is not the middle size times the middle rate. Read each as “typically about this”, not as one worked example.
