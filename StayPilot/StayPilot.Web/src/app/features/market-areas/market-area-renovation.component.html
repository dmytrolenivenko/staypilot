Where the market pays you enough for taking the work on. The other screens find value; this is the one that tells you where to create it.

Discount is measured: what stock needing work actually sells for per m² against finished stock in the same place, from real adverts. Renovation stock means the advert says it needs work or its energy certificate is D or worse — the certificate carries most of it, because “needs renovation” is set on barely 1% of listings and a grade is measured rather than typed by an agent.

Cost is an estimate, and yours to set. The two are kept in separate columns and never merged into a single score, because one is measured and the other is a guess — when the guess is wrong you need to be able to see it.
